A growing number of AI tool companies are choosing Business Source License (BSL) 1.1 over traditional open-source licenses. The trend reflects a fundamental tension: how to be transparent and community-friendly while building a sustainable business.

## Who Uses BSL

- **Terraform** (HashiCorp/IBM) — switched from MPL to BSL
- **Sentry** — adopted FSL (similar concept)
- **AgentLimb** — BSL 1.1, free for personal use, converts to Apache 2.0 in 2030

## Why BSL Works

BSL lets you:
- **Read, modify, and redistribute** the source code
- **Use it personally** for free
- **Prevent competitors** from reselling your work as a service

After the Change Date (typically 3-4 years), the code converts to a fully permissive license (Apache 2.0 or MIT).

## The Debate

Critics say BSL isn't "true open source" by OSI definitions. Proponents argue it's more honest than MIT-licensed projects that later close-source when they need revenue.

For users, BSL offers the transparency of open source with the guarantee that the project can sustain itself financially.
