## Why This Works Better Than Traditional Scraping

Most automated research tools use separate browser sessions that websites recognize and block. AgentLimb uses your real Chrome — the same browser you use every day — so websites see a normal visitor, not a bot.

More importantly, you're already logged in. If a competitor's pricing page is behind a signup wall, your AI can access it because you have an account. No special configuration needed.

---

## Tracking Competitor Pricing

Tell your AI what you want:

> *"Visit these three pricing pages and note the plan names, prices, and key features: competitor-a.com/pricing, competitor-b.com/pricing, competitor-c.com/pricing. Give me a side-by-side comparison."*

Your AI navigates to each page, reads the content, and gives you a summary. The first time it visits each site, it learns the page layout. Future checks are faster because the navigation is already known.

Set this up as a weekly task and you'll always have a current picture of the competitive landscape.

---

## Monitoring Brand Mentions

Tell your AI:

> *"Search Reddit for posts mentioning [competitor name] from the last week. List the titles, subreddits, and a brief summary of the sentiment in each."*

Your AI navigates to Reddit, runs the search, reads through the results, and gives you a summary. Because you're using your real browser with your account, you see the same results you'd see manually — including subreddits that require membership to view.

---

## Saving Research for Later

Your AI can collect information and summarize it in a message, which you can copy and store wherever you keep notes. For ongoing research, consider asking your AI to format the output consistently each time — the same columns, the same structure — so you can compare results across weeks.

---

## After the First Research Session

Once your AI has visited each competitor's site, it remembers the navigation patterns. Follow-up research sessions are significantly faster because your AI already knows how to find the pricing page, where to look for the key data, and what the page structure looks like.

If a competitor updates their website, your AI adapts automatically and updates its knowledge.

---

## Your Research Stays Private

Everything happens on your machine. No data goes through third-party scraping services. The competitor sites see a normal browser visit from your IP. Your findings stay on your computer.

---

## What You Can Track

- Pricing changes
- Feature additions or removals
- Job postings (hiring signals)
- Blog and content updates
- Social media activity and community mentions
- Product reviews and user feedback on forums

Anything you can read manually in a browser, your AI can read and summarize with AgentLimb.
