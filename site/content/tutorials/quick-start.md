## The Short Version

Three steps. No configuration. No code. No API keys.

**Step 1 — Install the extension.** Go to the Chrome Web Store and add AgentLimb to Chrome. One click. Done. (Or download the zip from GitHub and load it manually in Developer Mode if you prefer.)

**Step 2 — Start the bridge.** The bridge is a small background program that connects your AI to the extension. On Mac, run the install script once and it starts automatically every time you open your computer. On other systems, start it with a single command once per session.

**Step 3 — Copy the prompt and paste it to your AI.** Open the AgentLimb side panel in Chrome, click "Copy Onboard Prompt", and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Windsurf, or any AI tool you use. The AI reads it, connects to the bridge, and is ready to work. The whole connection takes a few seconds.

That's it. You can now give your AI browser tasks.

---

## What Happens When You Give the AI a Task

Tell your AI something like: *"Go to Reddit and post a message in r/test with the title 'Hello' and body 'Testing AgentLimb'."*

Your AI will:
- Announce its plan in the side panel (you can see the steps appear)
- Navigate to Reddit in your open Chrome tab
- Find the right buttons and fill in the form — using your existing Reddit login
- Submit the post
- Confirm it succeeded and close the task

You don't need to watch closely. The side panel shows each step as it happens.

---

## Your Logins Are Already There

AgentLimb uses your real Chrome — the browser you already have open with all your accounts. Reddit, GitHub, Notion, company tools — if you're already logged in, your AI can use them directly. No re-login, no cookie setup, no passwords to configure.

---

## The Muscle Memory Starts Immediately

After your AI completes its first task on a site, AgentLimb quietly saves what it learned about that site. Next time you run the same task, it skips the exploration and goes straight to the action. The second run is dramatically faster and cheaper.

A folder appears on your Desktop called **AgentLimb-muscle**. It holds all the knowledge your AI has accumulated. Don't delete it — that's your AI's long-term memory.
