GlotShot is the ultimate tool for indie developers who need to create app store screenshots in multiple languages — fast and privately.

## What It Does

Designing App Store preview images is tedious. You need different sizes for different devices, and if you want to localize, multiply that by every language. GlotShot automates the entire process.

- **Batch processing** — Generate screenshots for multiple devices and languages simultaneously
- **AI translation** — Built-in Ollama integration translates your copy into 12+ languages, all locally
- **Multi-store support** — iOS, Android, macOS, Windows Store, and Steam
- **Icon design** — Automatic cropping and resizing for all platform icon formats
- **Privacy first** — All processing runs locally, no cloud uploads

## The AgentLimb Connection

GlotShot handles your store assets. AgentLimb can automate the upload process — navigating App Store Connect, Google Play Console, and Steam partner sites to submit screenshots using your real login sessions.
