Three of the world's largest tech companies have entered the AI IDE market within months of each other:

- **Google** launched Firebase Studio — an AI-assisted full-stack app builder
- **AWS** released Kiro — a spec-driven AI IDE
- **ByteDance** introduced Trae — a free AI-powered code editor

## What It Means

When Big Tech enters a market, it validates the category and accelerates innovation. But it also raises questions for independent tools like Cursor and Windsurf.

## The Opportunity for AgentLimb

More AI IDEs = more potential AgentLimb users. Each of these tools can connect to AgentLimb's bridge via MCP, extending their capabilities into the browser. AgentLimb's value proposition — browser automation with muscle memory — is complementary to any IDE, regardless of who makes it.

## Predictions

- AI IDEs will become free or near-free (subsidized by cloud platform revenue)
- MCP standardization will prevent vendor lock-in
- The real differentiation will be in the agent capabilities built on top of the IDE
