WitNote is a local-first AI writing companion for macOS, Windows, and Linux. With over 500 GitHub stars, it has become one of the most popular privacy-focused writing tools in the indie developer community.

## What Makes WitNote Different

Most AI writing tools require a cloud connection — your text goes to a server, gets processed, and comes back. WitNote flips this model: all AI processing happens on your device.

- **Multiple AI engines** — Choose between WebLLM (runs in browser), Ollama (local models), or cloud APIs when you prefer
- **Dual-pane editor** — Write and preview side by side
- **Smart autocomplete** — Context-aware suggestions that respect your writing style
- **Focus mode** — Distraction-free environment that adapts to your window

## Why Local Matters

Your writing is personal. Journals, drafts, research notes — these shouldn't live on someone else's server. WitNote guarantees your data never leaves your machine unless you explicitly choose a cloud API.

## How It Connects to AgentLimb

Both WitNote and AgentLimb share the same design philosophy: **local-first, privacy by architecture, no cloud dependency**. They're built by the same developer with the same values — your tools should work for you, not harvest your data.
