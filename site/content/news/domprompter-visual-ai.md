DOMPrompter is a macOS desktop app that solves a common problem: telling your AI code editor exactly what to change on a web page.

## The Problem

You're looking at a webpage and want to tell Cursor or Claude Code: "make this button wider and change the color." But screenshots are imprecise, and describing CSS selectors by hand is tedious.

## The Solution

DOMPrompter lets you:

1. **Select elements directly** — Click any element on the page, get its CSS selector instantly
2. **Visually adjust parameters** — Drag to resize, tweak colors, change spacing
3. **Add text annotations** — Write notes like "button too dark" or "increase padding"
4. **Auto-generate structured prompts** — One click exports a structured AI prompt with selectors, diffs, and intent

Paste the generated prompt into Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, or any AI coding tool — and it knows exactly what to change.

## How It Complements AgentLimb

DOMPrompter generates prompts for **code changes**. AgentLimb drives browsers for **automation**. Together, they cover the full spectrum: design your changes with DOMPrompter, automate your workflows with AgentLimb.
