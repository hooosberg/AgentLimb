TrekReel is a macOS app for hikers, runners, and cyclists who want to share their adventures as cinematic 3D animations.

## How It Works

1. **Import your GPX file** from any fitness tracker (Strava, Apple Watch, Garmin, etc.)
2. **TrekReel builds a 3D terrain** using real elevation data and satellite imagery
3. **Add keyframes and annotations** — mark camps, water sources, scenic viewpoints
4. **Export a cinematic video** with smooth camera movements and terrain fly-throughs

## Key Features

- **Real 3D terrain** — elevation data meets satellite imagery for authentic landscapes
- **Route storytelling** — add photo markers and text annotations at key points
- **Cinematic camera** — smooth interpolation creates film-quality fly-throughs
- **Privacy-focused** — all data stays local, offline map support included

Like all hooosberg products, TrekReel is local-first. Your GPS data, photos, and routes never leave your device. The same philosophy drives AgentLimb: **your data is your data**.
